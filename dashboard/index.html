Questions, answered.

Is HalalReady part of JAKIM?

No. It is an independent tool. JAKIM and the state authorities issue certificates.

Does it submit for me?

No. You review the pack and submit on MYeHALAL yourself.

Does the AI decide what is halal?

No. It flags problems with evidence. You confirm each one.

What do I need to start?

Supplier certificates, your ingredient list and a phone video of your premises.
